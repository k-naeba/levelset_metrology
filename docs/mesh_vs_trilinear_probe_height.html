Raising the probe line through the cube

The same case-5 cube as before (corners 0, 2 negative, inside; corners 1, 3 positive, outside; the whole top face, corners 4–7, fixed at +1, outside). Corner values stay fixed while the probe line itself moves: a diagonal segment from (0,0,z) to (1,1,z), with height z sweeping from the bottom face up toward the top. Drag s above 1 and a third regime appears near the bottom: not just a position mismatch, but a real disagreement over whether a crossing exists at all.
